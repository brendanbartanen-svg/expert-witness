# Interventions

Everything the host tells the witness during the run beyond PRE-REPORT.md and HOST.md, recorded
verbatim with what the witness did about it (SKILL.md, Interventions). The record exists for
accountability, not identification: names and identifiers inside the host's words are redacted
as marked below; nothing else is changed. One entry per
intervention, appended in order; entries are never edited or removed. Each entry:

    ## {timestamp, ISO 8601 with offset}
    **Verbatim:**
    > {the host's words, exactly as given, except that a personal name or an identifier
    > becomes [name] or [id], with the note "redacted by the witness"}
    **Kind:** {clarification of fact | change to a question | request to omit, soften, delay, or preview | stop}
    **Response:** {accepted | declined | complied}: {what was done, and where it is recorded}

A clarification of fact (including a file supplied during the run) is accepted, and every item
that uses it says so. A change to a question, and a request to omit, soften, delay, or preview,
are declined. An instruction to stop is followed. Entries follow, oldest first.
